Reels Maker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Reels Maker is a private desktop tool. Its only user is its author.

What it accesses. With the user's permission (TikTok Login Kit), it receives:
- the basic profile, used only to show the connected account name: scope user.info.basic;
- the right to upload videos to that account's drafts: scope video.upload.

Where data is kept. The access tokens are stored only on the user's own computer, encrypted with the operating system's protection. They are never sent anywhere except to TikTok's own API.

What is collected. Nothing. Reels Maker has no servers, analytics or tracking. It collects, sells and shares no personal data.

Removing access. Press «Отключить» in Reels Maker to delete the tokens from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time in the TikTok app: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Contact: through the author's TikTok or Instagram profile.
