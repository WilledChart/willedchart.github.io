Reels Maker — Terms of Service

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Reels Maker is a private desktop tool. Its only user is its author, a music producer. It turns his own Ableton projects into short vertical videos of his own beats. It can upload those videos to his own TikTok account as drafts, using TikTok's official Content Posting API.

  1. Who may use it. Reels Maker is not offered to the public. Only the author uses it, and only with accounts he owns.
  2. Content. Every video uploaded with Reels Maker is made from the author's own music and project files. The author is responsible for it and for following TikTok's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
  3. TikTok access. Reels Maker uploads a video to TikTok only when the user presses the upload button. Videos go to the TikTok inbox (drafts). The user reviews and posts them in the TikTok app.
  4. No warranty. The tool is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.
  5. Changes. These terms may change. The current version is always on this page.
